## 2. Tone of voice
**What the site does** [Published]:
- Plain, informational and benefit-led, usually as short bullet lists followed by a direct call to action.
- Recurring themes: safety and security of money, convenience, responsible lending, improving livelihoods.
- Sample lines: "Convenient, fast and secure." · "Your money will be kept safely and securely." · "Looking for loan advice? Talk to our loan advisors!" · "LOLC Cambodia strongly upholds the principles of responsible lending."
- Brand promise in the footer: "Our products and services are committed to improving the livelihood of Cambodian customers."

**How we should write for LOLC** [Inferred]:
- **Trustworthy before clever.** Warm, respectful and clear. The audience includes rural families, farmers, micro-business owners and salaried workers, not only urban digital users.
- **English:** plain international English, short sentences, active voice. The live site has some non-native phrasing ("Though ATM Machine 24/7"). Don't copy those errors; write correct English and keep their product names exactly.
- **Khmer:** natural, polite, contemporary Khmer. Address customers respectfully (លោកអ្នក, as the site does), avoid slang, and avoid stiff legal Khmer outside the disclaimer itself. Check Khmer copy with a native reviewer at LOLC before release.
- Lead with the customer's goal (a shop, a harvest, school fees, savings for a child), then the product, then the condition.
- Calls to action should invite advice or information ("Talk to our loan officer", "See the full terms") rather than push borrowing ("Borrow now!").

## 5. Compliance cautions (read before any copy, VO or layout)
LOLC is an NBC-licensed, deposit-taking microfinance institution, and the Cambodian microfinance sector is under public scrutiny for over-indebtedness. These rules apply to every LOLC deliverable. Points marked [Inferred] are good practice for regulated financial advertising, not legal advice; LOLC's compliance team has the final say.

### Never
1. **No guaranteed-return or no-risk claims.** Don't write "guaranteed", "risk-free", "100% safe" or "best rate" about deposits or returns. The site says "Get a high interest income"; don't escalate that into promises. [Inferred]
2. **No deposit-protection claims.** No deposit-insurance or protection-scheme wording appears on LOLC's site. Don't imply deposits are insured or government-backed unless LOLC supplies the approved wording. [Published absence, Inferred rule]
3. **No "easy money" framing.** Avoid "instant cash", "no questions asked", "approved for everyone", "borrow for anything". Don't show borrowing for impulse spending or as a fix for existing debt. [Inferred]
4. **Don't promise approval.** Loans are "subject to approval and LOLC's credit assessment". [Inferred]
5. **Don't treat collateral lightly.** Don't use land titles, homes or gold as props in a casual or celebratory way. Collateral is a sensitive subject in Cambodian microfinance. [Inferred]
6. **No unsupported superlatives.** "Leading" is LOLC's own published claim; "No. 1", "biggest" or "cheapest" need a cited source from LOLC. [Inferred]

### Always
1. **State rates clearly, with their basis.** The site quotes loan rates **per month** (for example Group Loan "1.5%", SME Loan "1.2%–1.5%") and savings rates **per year**. Every rate in artwork must say *per month* or *per year*, the currency, and any range ("from 1.2% per month"). Never show a monthly rate where it could be read as annual. [Published rates, Inferred rule]
2. **Show fees with rates.** Loan pages publish processing or approval fees (for example 1%–8% per cycle depending on product). If an ad mentions a rate, include the main fee or a clear pointer to the full fee schedule. [Published]
3. **Get every number from LOLC for each campaign.** The public pages contradict themselves (savings 0.25%/4.00% in the table vs 3%/1% in the summary; different loan terms and fees between summary and body on Group Loan, SME Loan and WIL). Never copy a rate, term, limit or fee from the website into final work. Ask LOLC for the current, approved figure and keep a note of who approved it and when. [Published conflict]
4. **Include the conditions line.** For example: "Terms and conditions apply. LOLC reserves the right to change any terms and conditions without prior notice." (the second sentence is LOLC's own wording). Ask LOLC for the approved Khmer version. [Published]
5. **"Free" needs its condition.** "Free life insurance up to USD 5,000" and "free of charge for any transactions" (LOLC Mobile) must keep their limits and the terms reference. [Published]
6. **Include the regulator line where LOLC asks for it**, for example "Licensed by the National Bank of Cambodia". Confirm the approved wording for each format. [Inferred]
7. **Promotions need an end date and conditions.** [Inferred]
8. **Promote responsible borrowing.** Where space allows, use messages like "borrow what you can repay" or "talk to a loan officer first". This matches LOLC's published "responsible lending" stance and its M-CRIL client protection certification. [Inferred]
9. **Use real or consented people.** Testimonials and customer photos need consent and must not overstate results. Don't invent customer stories. [Inferred]

### Regulatory context to verify with LOLC [Inferred]
- NBC rules on interest-rate caps for microfinance lending, and on transparency and disclosure of rates and fees to customers, apply. Confirm the current requirements, and the exact disclosure wording LOLC uses, with their compliance team before the first campaign.
